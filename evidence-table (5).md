## Evidence Synthesis

The two studies retained in `03_final_included.csv` provide applied background evidence on black-box test design with EP/BVA and Decision Table Testing. SS001 reports scenario-level results and validation defects; SS084 reports issues found while testing a pension-claim form. Neither study compares students using EP-BVA and Decision-Table worksheets on the same 20 PURE requirements against an instructor reference inventory. The current evidence therefore does not answer which worksheet achieves higher item coverage; that outcome must come from the review's own experiment.

## Research Gap

The included evidence does not directly test the target population, worksheet comparison, or item-coverage outcome. A controlled study using the same 20 frozen PURE requirements and a consistent instructor reference inventory is still needed to compare the two worksheets.
